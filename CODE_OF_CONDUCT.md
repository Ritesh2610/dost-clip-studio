# Code of conduct

This is a small project with one maintainer. These are the rules for taking part in it: in issues, pull requests, reviews and any other space the project uses.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are new to the code, to video tools or to English.
- Talk about the work, not the person. "This function drops the last second of audio" helps. "You clearly did not test this" does not.
- Give reasons with your opinions, and accept that the maintainer makes the final call on what goes into the project.
- Say so when you are wrong or unsure. Nobody loses standing here for that.
- Respect people's time. Search existing issues before opening a new one, and give enough detail for someone else to reproduce a problem.
- Respect creators. Do not ask for help clipping videos you do not have permission to use.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, mockery or personal attacks.
- Harassment of any kind, including unwelcome sexual attention, stalking and repeated contact after someone has asked you to stop.
- Hateful or demeaning remarks about someone's gender, sexuality, race, caste, religion, nationality, language, age, disability, body or level of experience.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address, phone number or private video link, without their permission.
- Posting other people's videos, music or other copyrighted material in issues or pull requests.
- Deliberately derailing a discussion, or spam.

## How to report a problem

Do not reply in public to the person involved, and do not open a public issue about it.

Report it privately to the maintainer, [@Ritesh2610](https://github.com/Ritesh2610), through GitHub:

1. Open the menu (the three dots) on the comment, issue or pull request, choose "Report content" and pick the option that sends the report to the repository's maintainers. Only the maintainer sees these reports.
2. If that option is not offered, or the problem is with the maintainer, send the report to GitHub instead. Use the same "Report content" menu, or the form at <https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse>.

Say what happened, where (a link is best) and when. Reports are kept private. The maintainer will not share who made a report without that person's agreement.

## What happens next

The maintainer reads every report and decides what to do. Depending on how serious it is, that can be a private word, a warning, removing or editing comments, or blocking someone from the project. The maintainer will tell the person who reported it what was decided when it is possible to do so.

## Where this applies

These rules apply in this repository and anywhere else someone is acting on behalf of the project.
